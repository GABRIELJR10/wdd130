Our Rafting Trips

White Water Adventure

Experience an exciting white water rafting adventure through challenging river rapids. Learn basic rafting techniques, follow your guide’s safety instructions, and enjoy an unforgettable outdoor experience. Helmets, life jackets, and paddles can be provided for your trip.

Scenic River Trip

Enjoy a peaceful journey along a scenic river surrounded by beautiful natural landscapes. Take time to appreciate nature, observe the riverbanks, and relax during this memorable outdoor adventure. This trip is ideal for visitors who prefer a calm river experience.

Group Rafting Experience

Enjoy an exciting rafting experience with friends, family, or other adventurers. Work together as a team, learn basic paddling techniques, and follow the guide’s safety instructions as you explore the river. This trip is a great opportunity to build teamwork and create unforgettable memories together.
